Recorded walkthrough. This page replays the real outputs from my prototype runs on 5 November 2026 (prototype v1.1, prompt v2.1), so it works without signing in or calling an AI model. Every check, gate, Legal step and log entry runs live in your browser on those recorded outputs. The live agent is at claude.ai (Claude sign-in needed).
  1. Golden brief: Load golden brief → Run workflow. In Node 4b, confirm “handmade” with a source (the brief says “hand-thrown”) → Approve.
  2. Edge case: Load edge case → Run workflow. In Node 4, refer “Calm” (reason) and “clinically proven” (reason + evidence), then Confirm all remaining removals → Send to Legal.
  3. Legal: in the Legal panel, approve “calm”, decline “clinically proven” → Submit. Pass 2 re-runs; Approve stays locked (unsupported facts, missing dimensions) → Reject.
